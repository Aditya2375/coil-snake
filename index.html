A game with consequences

Eat: +10. Collision or timeout: −10. Each move: −0.03, plus ±0.08 for moving closer to or farther from food. Episodes stop after 100 moves without food or 1,500 total moves. Food on the final move still earns +10.

The learning loop

Double DQN with replay memory and a separate target network. Exploration falls from 100% to 4%. A batch of 64 past transitions updates the network every four steps; the target copies it every 400 steps. Discount factor 0.95, Adam step size 0.001.
